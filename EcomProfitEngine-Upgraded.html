Ecom founder reminders
01
Revenue is not profit
The number everyone brags about hides the number that actually matters. Know your profit per order before you celebrate a big month.
02
Fix margin before scaling ads
Scaling bad unit economics just loses money faster. Get to 20%+ margin before you increase paid spend significantly.
03
Returns destroy margin quietly
A 10% return rate can wipe out half your profit at thin margins. Product quality, sizing clarity, and photography reduce this before the sale happens.
04
COGS is your biggest lever
Negotiating your supplier down 10% often beats a 10% price increase because there's no resistance. Most founders never ask.
05
Upsells change everything
A $12 order bump at 20% take rate adds $2.40 to every order's blended profit. That can be the difference between a dead offer and a scalable one.
06
Email is your highest-margin channel
Owned channel CPA can be $1–5 vs $15–40 for paid social. Even 20% of revenue from email dramatically improves blended channel margin.
07
Q4 ad costs spike hard
October–December CPAs can rise 40–60% as brands compete. If your model only works at current CPAs, you have a fragile business, not a real one.
08
Know your break-even ROAS
If your break-even ROAS is 2.5x, you need every $1 in ads to return $2.50 in revenue just to not lose money. Your media buyer should know this number cold.
09
LTV changes the game
If you have 30% repeat purchase rate, you can afford a higher CPA on the first order because the second order costs almost nothing to acquire.
10
Cash flow ≠ profit
Inventory ties up cash before you earn revenue. A profitable business can still go under from poor cash timing. Model both separately.
